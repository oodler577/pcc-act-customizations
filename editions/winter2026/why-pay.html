PERL COMMUNITY CONFERENCE · WINTER 2026

Why do we require attendees to pay?

Our volunteers put in hundreds of hours each year organizing PCC. For Winter 2026, that means planning the program, coordinating with speakers, and running the conference in Austin and online. The $30 conference fee helps cover the expenses that come with putting on the event.

What the fee helps cover

  • Conference video production. Recording and editing talks, capturing slides and clear audio, and preparing videos for publication.
  • Crossref DOI registration. The conference fee helps fund DOI registration and maintaining records for eligible Science track talk videos, provided at no charge to speakers. The track accompanies SPJ papers, with a one-time exception per speaker for a talk without a paper or planned SPJ submission. Read the speaker and DOI policy.
  • Keynote speaker reimbursement. Helping cover expenses such as travel and lodging for keynote speakers.
  • Food and refreshments in Austin. Providing food and drinks for in-person attendees during the conference.
  • In-person supplies and audiovisual equipment. Microphones, displays, cables, and materials for talks and demonstrations.

Speakers with approved talks receive a free ticket to the whole conference.
