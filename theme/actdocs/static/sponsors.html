SPONSOR PCC · SUPPORT PRACTICAL PERL

A modest contribution. A meaningful difference.

Help Perl developers, researchers, students, and maintainers share useful work. We organize two Perl Community Conferences a year and support the recordings, publications, and online resources that keep that knowledge available afterward.

Our volunteers provide many hundreds of hours of work. Sponsorship helps cover professional video services for the conferences, Crossref DOI registration and journal publishing tools, and hosting for OJS, the journal, and the conference site.

$1,000 would make a real difference to our year.

Our baseline annual fundraising goal is at least $1,000 to help defray these costs. One Diamond PERL sponsor, two Platinum PERL sponsors, or four Golden PERL sponsors could meet that goal. Smaller contributions combine to do the same useful work.

Recognizing our supporters

We want to thank sponsors visibly and generously. Possible recognition includes names and logos on the site, acknowledgments during conferences, and thanks alongside recordings and announcements. The recognition program is being developed; placements and benefits will be agreed with organizers before a sponsorship is finalized.

No sponsor roster is listed yet. We would be glad to discuss a suitable arrangement and how you would like your support acknowledged, including whether you prefer anonymity.
