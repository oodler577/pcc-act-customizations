Perl 5 talks, projects,

The Perl Community Conference meets twice a year, around Perl’s birthday on December 18 and around July 4, bringing Perl programmers, project maintainers, and researchers together in Austin, Texas and online. PCC combines general Perl talks and lightning talks with the Science Perl track.

RESEARCH & PUBLICATION

Science Perl

The Science Perl track connects conference presentations with the Science Perl Journal. Calls for papers have included full papers, short papers, and extended abstracts alongside general Perl talks that require no paper.

PCC TALKS & RECORDINGS

What the Perl community is working on.

Past PCC programs have covered Catalyst and Valiant, PAGI and ASGI, wxPerl on Windows, Perl data types, DevOps, Beowulf clusters, and scientific algorithms. Conference recordings are published on The Perl Community YouTube channel.
