Recordings that keep the work accessible

The three inventoried PCC playlists contain 41 recordings totaling 21 hours, 27 minutes, and 31 seconds. This includes reports and opening/closing segments; it is recording time, not a technical-talk-hours total. It includes recordings supplied in an unlisted collection, so it is not a count of publicly released videos.

Six additional Las Vegas Science Perl recordings are linked in our archive; their running time is not included above. The curated Past Talks page currently features 27 recordings. Playlist catalogues and the curated selection measure different things.

People contributing research and practical experience

At least 31 named people appear across the published technical programs, the Science Perl track/SPJ author list, and the documented externship presentation. A person appearing at several events is counted once. Unnamed panelists, co-presenters, volunteers, reviewers, and collaborators are not added to the total.

How to read these numbers

This is a documented minimum, not a complete census of everyone’s contribution. The sources are the four linked conference schedules, the three PCC recording collections, the Science Perl Journal, and its published Science Perl track list. Talk counts, recording counts, hours, people, papers, and projects have separate definitions; they should not be added together into one total.
