SUPPORT PCC · THE PERL COMMUNITY · SCIENCE PERL

Help keep useful Perl knowledge within everyone’s reach.

Our volunteers contribute many hundreds of hours each year to organize the Perl Community Conference, prepare recordings, publish the Science Perl Journal, and keep our shared resources running. Your donation helps pay the bills that volunteer time alone cannot cover.

Our annual fundraising goal: at least $1,000

Each conference costs hundreds of dollars to produce, and publishing the journal also costs hundreds. Raising at least $1,000 a year helps defray these expenses and sustain the work throughout the year.

Donations are accepted through RoboPerl, our parent 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

What your support makes possible

  • Professional video services. We believe in high-quality recordings and want to keep improving them. Professional recording and editing help us deliver clearer audio, synchronized slides and screen recordings, and more consistent releases. They also let volunteers put more of their time into the next conference.
  • Journal publishing services. Donations help cover preparation and publication of the Science Perl Journal, including DOI registration through Crossref and tools such as Overleaf that support our publishing workflow.
  • Web hosting. Keeping the conference site, journal, and supporting online services available takes dependable infrastructure and ongoing funding.

Small gifts make a practical difference

A gift of $10, $25, $50, or any amount you can comfortably give helps. Our $1,000 annual goal is a starting point for defraying costs, not a claim that it covers every expense. Further support helps sustain and improve the conferences, recordings, journal, and infrastructure.

Explore Past Talks to see the work our speakers share, or visit Impacts for our documented programs, contributors, recordings, and publications.

Giving through our nonprofit

RoboPerl—Public Enrichment & Robotics Laboratories—is our parent 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations through RoboPerl support this nonprofit work. Its support page provides additional donation methods and information for your records.

Thank you for supporting the people who share their work and for helping preserve useful Perl knowledge for the next person.

Stay in touch: join the Science Perl mailing list for PCC news, calls for participation, and recording releases.
